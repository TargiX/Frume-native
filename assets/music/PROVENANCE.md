# Music provenance

These six instrumental tracks were generated by the Frume project owner with
Suno. On 2026-08-01 the owner confirmed that all six generations were created
under their paid Suno Pro plan and approved their use in Frume.

This manifest records the shipped files; it is not a substitute for the
private subscription receipt/export evidence that must be retained with the
release records. Do not commit account credentials or billing documents.

The source MP3 exports were converted to AAC/M4A and loudness-normalized to
approximately -18 LUFS for consistent, unobtrusive in-game playback. Embedded
Suno generation IDs and creation timestamps were retained.
